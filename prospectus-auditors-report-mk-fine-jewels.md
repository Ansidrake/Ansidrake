**Issuer:** M.K.Fine Jewels Ltd (no subsidiary, joint venture or associate)

**Basis:** Illustrative format in **Appendix 4** to the **Guidance Note on Reports in Company Prospectuses (Revised 2019)** (ICAI). Bracketed text and note references to be finalised for the actual engagement.

**Scope note (Ind AS restatement without re-audit):** Restated financial information in a prospectus is covered by an **examination** engagement under the Guidance Note, not by re-auditing prior-period statutory financial statements. It is **permissible** for the current auditor to report on the **Restated Standalone Financial Information** for March 31, 2023 and March 31, 2024 **without re-auditing** those years, provided sufficient appropriate evidence is obtained on the **restatement adjustments** (here, **Ind AS-based** recognition and measurement of **employee retirement benefits** under **Ind AS 19** and any other **retrospective Ind AS alignment** described in the notes). The paragraph below (after paragraph 6) makes that scope explicit. **Peer review / ICDR / engagement-letter requirements** must still be satisfied separately.
