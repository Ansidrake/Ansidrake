## Report on the Restated Standalone Financial Information

We have examined the attached **Restated Standalone Financial Information** of **M.K.Fine Jewels Ltd** (“the Company”), prepared in connection with the proposed issue / offer document (as described in the offer document), comprising:

- the Restated Standalone Balance Sheet as at 31 March 2023, 31 March 2024, 31 March 2025 and 31 December 2025;
- the Restated Standalone Statement of Profit and Loss;
- the Restated Standalone Statement of Cash Flows; and
- the Restated Standalone Statement of Changes in Equity;

for the years ended 31 March 2023, 31 March 2024 and 31 March 2025, and for the nine months ended 31 December 2025,

together with the **Summary of Significant Accounting Policies** and other explanatory information, in each case as approved by the Board of Directors / audit committee (as applicable) and initialed by us for identification.

The Restated Standalone Financial Information has been prepared by the Company’s Management in accordance with the requirements of:

- **Section 26** of Part I of Chapter III of the **Companies Act, 2013** as amended from time to time (the “Act”);
- the **Securities and Exchange Board of India (Issue of Capital and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2018**, as amended (“SEBI ICDR Regulations”), to the extent applicable; and
- the **Guidance Note on Reports in Company Prospectuses (Revised 2019)** issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (“ICAI”).

The Company does not have any subsidiary, joint venture or associate company. Accordingly, the Restated Standalone Financial Information is **standalone** financial information.

### Responsibilities of Management for the Restated Standalone Financial Information

The Company’s Board of Directors is responsible for the preparation of the Restated Standalone Financial Information in accordance with the requirements of Section 26 of the Act, the SEBI ICDR Regulations (as applicable) and the Guidance Note on Reports in Company Prospectuses referred to above. This responsibility includes designing, implementing and maintaining internal control relevant to the preparation of the Restated Standalone Financial Information, and applying an appropriate basis of preparation and making accounting estimates that are reasonable in the circumstances.

### Auditor’s Responsibilities

Our responsibility is to express an opinion on the Restated Standalone Financial Information based on our procedures.

We conducted our engagement in accordance with the **Standard on Related Services (SRS) 4400 (Revised)**, *Engagements to Perform Agreed-Upon Procedures Regarding Financial Information*, as issued by the ICAI, to the extent applicable, and the Guidance Note on Reports in Company Prospectuses (Revised 2019). An engagement performed pursuant to the Guidance Note on Reports in Company Prospectuses and SRS 4400 (Revised) involves performing procedures to obtain sufficient appropriate evidence as the basis for our opinion on the Restated Standalone Financial Information. The procedures selected depend on the auditor’s judgement. Our procedures included:

- examining, on a test basis, evidence supporting the amounts and disclosures in the Restated Standalone Financial Information;
- performing procedures to reconcile the Restated Standalone Financial Information to the underlying audited financial statements (and, where applicable, limited reviewed interim financial information) from which it has been derived; and
- performing such other procedures as we considered necessary in the circumstances.

We believe that the evidence we have obtained is sufficient and appropriate to provide a basis for our opinion.

### Opinion

In our opinion and to the best of our information and according to the explanations given to us:

- the Restated Standalone Financial Information, read with the Summary of Significant Accounting Policies and notes thereon, **gives a true and fair view** in conformity with the accounting principles generally accepted in India; and
- the Restated Standalone Financial Information has been prepared **in accordance with Section 26** of Part I of Chapter III of the Act and the SEBI ICDR Regulations (as applicable), and the Guidance Note on Reports in Company Prospectuses (Revised 2019) issued by the ICAI.

### Other Matter — Financial information for periods audited by the predecessor auditor

The Restated Standalone Financial Information for the **years ended 31 March 2023 and 31 March 2024** included in this report (or annexed hereto) is based on the **standalone financial statements** of the Company for those years, which were audited by **M/s. Parmar and Co**, Chartered Accountants, who expressed **modified (qualified) opinions** on those financial statements.

The **modifications** in the audit reports issued by the predecessor auditor for both years related to **non-provision of employee retirement benefits** in the standalone financial statements for those years, in accordance with the applicable Indian Accounting Standards / accounting standards (as applicable).

The Company has **restated** the comparative financial information for those periods to give effect to the **provision for employee retirement benefits** and related adjustments, as more fully described in **Note [●]** to the Restated Standalone Financial Information. **Management has recognised the appropriate provision (and related adjustments) in the restatement** to address the matters that formed the basis of the predecessor auditor’s modifications.

Our opinion on the Restated Standalone Financial Information **is not modified** in respect of the above matter.

### Other Matter — Financial information for periods audited by us

The standalone financial statements of the Company for the **year ended 31 March 2025** and for the **nine months ended 31 December 2025** were audited by **us, M/s. Paresh Rakesh and Associates**, Chartered Accountants. We issued **unmodified** auditor’s reports on those financial statements / financial results (as applicable).

The Restated Standalone Financial Information for these periods has been prepared by restating the said audited financial statements, after incorporating the effects of the restatement adjustments described in the Summary of Significant Accounting Policies and notes to the Restated Standalone Financial Information, including alignment of accounting policies and retrospective adjustments on a consistent basis, where applicable.

Our opinion on the Restated Standalone Financial Information **is not modified** in respect of this matter.
